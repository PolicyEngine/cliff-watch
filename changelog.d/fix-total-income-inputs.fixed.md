Send household inputs to the policyengine-us variables that rules read instead of totals and derived variables, which left those rules seeing zero. Pensions now reach AGI and income taxes (`taxable_private_pension_income`, plus a new government pension field on `taxable_public_pension_income`). Dividends get qualified rates (`qualified_dividend_income`), and Social Security reaches rules that read `social_security_retirement`. Savings count toward TANF and SSI asset tests (`bank_account_assets`, which go to the head, or to the spouse when only the spouse is 65 or older or blind), and utility bills earn the SNAP utility allowance (`heating_cooling_expense`). TANF phases out when a second adult has wages: a `tanf_gross_earned_income` override had frozen it across the earnings axis. The single-household endpoint now matches the earnings series, because it no longer overrides MAGI, which had left out Social Security. The Dividends field becomes Qualified dividends, Pension becomes Private pension, and Social Security becomes Social Security retirement; share links made before this release keep their amounts under those narrower meanings. Adds fast-check, Hypothesis and live-API invariant tests of the payload builders.
