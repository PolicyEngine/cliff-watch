CliffWatch now runs on `calculate_household` from policyengine.py, whose `us` extra sets the engine: policyengine-us 2.2.1, policyengine-core 3.33.3 and spm-calculator 1.0.0. SNAP and WIC amounts now sum the calendar year rather than multiplying January by twelve, so they include each October's cost-of-living change. A household needs someone aged 18 or older, and years outside 2022 to 2035 now raise, because the engine's default outputs measure SPM poverty.
