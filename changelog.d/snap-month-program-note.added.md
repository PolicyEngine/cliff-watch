Add a note under the cliff chart where a household gets a full year of Head Start, Early Head Start or free school meals only through SNAP in part of the year. Each `/api/series` point now lists the months the household gets SNAP (`snap_months`) and the programs it gets for the whole year only through those months (`part_year_snap_programs`).
